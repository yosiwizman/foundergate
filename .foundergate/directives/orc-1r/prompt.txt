PHASE 6 — EFFECT-AWARE RECOVERY LAW

Every recoverable action must carry exactly one effect class:

READ_ONLY
REVERSIBLE_LOCAL
REPOSITORY_MUTATION
EXTERNAL_IDEMPOTENT
EXTERNAL_UNCERTAIN
IRREVERSIBLE

Create a single authoritative effect-class registry or schema integrated with the existing PAES, ARE and held-action path.

Rules:

READ_ONLY:
- may rerun after state validation.

REVERSIBLE_LOCAL:
- may resume or rerun only after rollback and duplicate checks.

REPOSITORY_MUTATION:
- must reconcile worktree, branch, commit, remote ancestry, PR and evidence before retry.

EXTERNAL_IDEMPOTENT:
- may retry only when:
  - a valid idempotency key exists;
  - external-state reconciliation proves the effect is absent or safely repeatable;
  - current authority permits the action.

EXTERNAL_UNCERTAIN:
- must park;
- must route a Founder Decision Inbox gate;
- must not retry automatically.

IRREVERSIBLE:
- must never retry automatically;
- must route a founder or human-expert gate.

UNKNOWN OR MISSING EFFECT CLASS:
FAIL_CLOSED
PARK_ACTION
ROUTE_GATE

The existing held-action sink remains the only authorized external-action boundary.

No alternate executor, retry path, recovery helper or worker may bypass it.

Required negative proof:

- unknown class cannot execute;
- external-uncertain cannot retry;
- irreversible cannot retry;
- a worker cannot relabel an effect to avoid the gate;
- a completed action cannot execute twice;
- a missing idempotency key blocks external-idempotent retry.
