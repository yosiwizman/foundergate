FOUNDERGATE ARLC-1 — PERMANENT AUTONOMOUS REPOSITORY LIFECYCLE CONTROLLER, CURRENT STACK CLOSURE, AND GATE-TO-GATE CONTINUATION

FOUNDER AUTHORIZATION

I establish the Autonomous Repository Lifecycle Law as a permanent FounderGate operating rule.

Routine mechanically qualified repository operations are backend Terminal-0 responsibilities, not founder gates.

FounderGate must autonomously discover, verify, execute, reconcile and close routine repository lifecycle work without requiring the founder or Advisor to remember to paste another prompt.

The founder must be surfaced only for:

- human-perception UI witnesses;
- legal, security, tax, IP, payment, health or regulatory judgment;
- provider adoption or default-provider selection;
- private repository, customer-data or production-data authority;
- public deployment or release;
- irreversible deletion or migration;
- spending beyond approved budgets;
- live external actions;
- policy weakening;
- a genuinely unresolved strategic decision.

Routine commits, PR operations, mechanically qualified merges, CI reruns, checker correction cycles, superseded-PR closure, branch cleanup, worktree cleanup, lane continuation and safe phase transitions are not founder gates.

This authorization supersedes earlier disk rules that required founder approval for every main merge or prohibited Terminal-0 from executing an otherwise mechanically qualified merge.

It does not weaken evidence, checker, Advisor, exact-SHA, qualification, rollback, data, security or active-lock requirements.

It does not authorize OpenSandbox adoption, customer or private repository execution, production deployment, public release, AKIOR access, payments, external actions or policy weakening.

ARCHITECTURAL VERDICT

Do not implement one omnipotent auto-merge agent.

Implement separation of duties:

- MOD-001 FounderGate Core loads and enforces the active lifecycle law.
- MOD-013 Branch/PR/Lane Orchestrator discovers and queues lifecycle work.
- MOD-018 Integration Merge Gate derives eligibility.
- MOD-022 GitHub Connector performs bounded remote operations.
- MOD-015 Audit/Proof Ledger records actions and outcomes.
- MOD-014 Gate/Witness Engine emits true founder gates.
- Terminal-0 is the sole sequential executor.
- Builders may not approve or merge their own work.
- Checkers may not execute merges.
- The connector may not decide eligibility.
- The orchestrator may not grant authority.
- The audit ledger may not approve.
- No component may self-certify the action it performed.

PHASE 10 — NO-LOSS CLEANUP ENGINE

- its full head is reachable from verified main;
- no unique required commit remains outside main;
- its evidence is preserved;
- no active lane depends on it;
- no unresolved finding requires the PR to remain open.

A remote branch may be deleted only when:

- all commits are reachable from verified main or a preserved evidence branch;
- no open non-superseded PR uses it;
- no active worktree uses it;
- it is not protected;
- it is not an M1 PASS evidence branch;
- it is not a prior NOT-PASSED evidence branch;
- rollback requirements are satisfied.

A worktree may be removed only when:

- no process uses it;
- it is not current;
- it has no unique uncommitted or untracked files;
- all commits are preserved remotely;
- governed evidence is archived;
- recovery state does not reference it.

Deletion decisions must be individually logged.
