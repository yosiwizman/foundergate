# Known Limitations
- A small demonstration module — a task ledger with status + evidence notes. Not production software.
- In-memory only; no persistence beyond reading a JSON file; no database.
- No network, no auth, no multi-user, no concurrency handling.
- Synthetic data only — no real people or customer data.
- Not production-ready, not customer-ready, not secure, not compliant, not deployed — and none of those are claimed.
