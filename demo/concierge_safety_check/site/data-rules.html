Data rules (hard)

  • Public, non-sensitive repos only. No secrets, credentials, tokens, customer/private/production data.
  • Nothing leaves the machine except the evidence note. No external sends, no analytics, no telemetry.
  • Sensitive content → we stop and decline. No exceptions during the concierge phase.
  • No credentials requested or stored. No key enrollment.

No-secrets policy

A secret scan runs before every run. A real credential hit stops the run and the repo is declined. The evidence note itself is scanned before handback.

Internal demo. Public non-sensitive repos only. No secrets. Not a production or security certification. No customers, revenue, or design partners are claimed.
