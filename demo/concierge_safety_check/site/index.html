What this is

A human-operated (concierge) pilot. You give one public, non-sensitive repository and a small objective. FounderGate makes a bounded change locally and returns a plain-English evidence note: the exact commit, what changed, the tests, an honest ship / do-not-ship / insufficient decision, and the limitations.

What it is not

Not a production audit. Not a security or compliance certification. Not a deployment. Not customer or private data handling.

The honest promise

  • Public, non-sensitive repos only — no secrets.
  • Nothing leaves your machine except the evidence note.
  • An honest “do-not-ship” is a valid outcome — it refuses to fake a success.
