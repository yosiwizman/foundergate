The offer

A free first concierge run on one public, non-sensitive repo, in exchange for honest feedback (and a reference if you are happy). You receive an auditable evidence note and a 20-minute walkthrough.

You receive

  • Plain-English evidence note (commit, tests, verdicts, honest decision, limits).
  • Independent Checker + Gate Advisor verdicts.
  • Optional sealed, checksummed local package.

You do NOT receive

No deployment · no merge into your repo · no security/compliance certification · no production-readiness guarantee · no handling of sensitive data.

Pricing

First run free. Any later pricing is an unvalidated hypothesis — no price is set, no willingness-to-pay is proven, no revenue or customers exist or are claimed.
