Overview

FounderGate is an evidence-first governance layer for autonomous AI development runs. It lets an AI build/check/review code while proving — from disk, git, and provider logs — exactly what it did, and refusing to over-claim.

This is a local internal demo. It is not production-ready, customer-ready, secure, compliant, or deployed. No real customer data is used. No customers or revenue exist or are claimed.

  • Autonomous endurance proven L1–L4 (2h/5h/8h) from disk logs.
  • Merges only behind an explicit founder gate.
  • Two bounded real AI-provider runs under a hard cost cap (~$1 total), with honest conservative decisions.
