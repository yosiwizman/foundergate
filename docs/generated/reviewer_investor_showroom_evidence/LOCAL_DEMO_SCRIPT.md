# Local Demo Script (5–7 minutes, for Yossi)
**Open:** the local dashboard → click "🚗 Founder Drive Mode" (top of sidebar).

1. **(1 min) Founder Drive Mode.** Say: "This is the simple view. It tells me what just happened, whether it passed, what's blocked, and one next step — no code, no terminal." Point at the safety labels (GATE 8 BLOCKED, NEVER SHIP) and the status cards.
2. **(1 min) Gate 8 card + What next.** Say: "Gate 8 — showing this to an outside person — is blocked until I choose to unlock it. My next step is just to open the reviewer view."
3. **(1 min) Reviewer Readiness → Evidence index.** Click "Open reviewer view." Say: "Here's the proof timeline and every sealed evidence package with two independent reviewer verdicts. Notice most are NOT yet founder-accepted — I'm being honest about that."
4. **(1 min) Crash Test.** Click "Crash Test" in the sidebar, "Load crash test." Say: "I deliberately broke it 12 ways. Every broken case is red and blocked from previewing. It recovered 6 safely and kept 6 held — it never faked a fix."
5. **(1 min) Claims summary + What is not proven.** Back in Reviewer Readiness, point at the claims table and the 'What is not proven' list. Say: "Green means local synthetic only. No customer, no payment, not production."
6. **(30 sec) Close.** Say: "That's the honest state. The next real test is one feedback session — and that needs my explicit go-ahead."

**What NOT to say:** "It's production ready" · "customers use it" · "it's SaaS" · "someone paid" · "it's secure/compliant" · any revenue or sales claim.
