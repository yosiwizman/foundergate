# Why AKIOR Second (or not)
FOR (second): cross-account proof shows portability of the governed workflow across a founder-controlled account — a stronger technical story once you have a public demo to anchor it.
AGAINST (first): it needs a founder-controlled account + a login/external action (a gate), and it proves portability, NOT customer/market validation. Doing it before a public demo gives you less to point at.
Net: recommended second, after Publish Demo — or first only if you specifically want the technical-portability story before the demo.
