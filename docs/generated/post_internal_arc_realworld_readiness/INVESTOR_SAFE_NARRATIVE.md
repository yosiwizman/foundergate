# Investor-Safe Narrative
FounderGate is an evidence-first governance layer for autonomous AI development. What we can show today, honestly:
- A non-developer founder can run a governed check with 3 clicks and no terminal, and get a sealed, tamper-evident evidence trail whose decision never says "ship."
- The same discipline reproduces across accounts and repeats without flake, and it caught every defect we injected.
- We proved we can use a real provider under a strict, exact-measured cost ceiling.
What we are NOT claiming: production readiness, security/compliance, customers, revenue, or a live SaaS. Those are deliberately separate, gated milestones we have not taken. The value on offer is honesty + reproducibility discipline, demonstrated on self-owned material.
