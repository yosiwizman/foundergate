# Gate 9 — Risk Register
| # | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | Selling on an overclaim | Claims matrix; informed-consent-to-limits required; refund if breached |
| 2 | Handling customer data to earn revenue | Not authorized; separate gate; refuse |
| 3 | Standing up billing prematurely | Payment/billing not authorized; separate gate |
| 4 | Counting non-revenue as revenue | Strict definition + "does not count" register |
| 5 | Compliance/tax exposure | Out of scope until a real, separately-gated commercial setup |
