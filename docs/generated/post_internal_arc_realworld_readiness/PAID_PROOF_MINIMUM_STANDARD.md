# Paid-Proof Minimum Standard
To count as Gate 9 paid proof (later, if authorized):
- A real external payer (not the founder, not a related party doing a favor).
- A real payment actually settled (not a promise, not an intent).
- Informed consent to the stated limits (no production/security/SaaS/customer-data claims).
- Honest recording: amount, date, what was delivered, and what was NOT claimed.
Anything short of all four does NOT count as paid proof.
