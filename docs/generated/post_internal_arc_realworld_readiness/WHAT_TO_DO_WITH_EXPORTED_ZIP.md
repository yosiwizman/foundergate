# What To Do With The Exported ZIP
- The exported ZIP is a sealed, tamper-evident record of what the local check did and tested.
- Keep it as your evidence trail. You do NOT need to open or read its contents.
- Only bring it back to ChatGPT when you want an independent review or to prepare a next decision.
- Do not email/send it to anyone as part of using the dashboard — sending it is a separate decision (outreach), not part of the flow.
