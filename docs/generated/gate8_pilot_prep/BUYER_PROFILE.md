# Buyer Profile (HYPOTHESIS — synthetic personas, no real targets)
These are hypothetical evaluator profiles for planning; no real people or companies are named, contacted, or profiled from private data.

## Persona A — "Careful engineering lead at a small AI-dev shop"
- Pain: autonomous coding agents produce plausible-but-unverified changes; hard to trust "done".
- Value hypothesis: an independent, hash-locked evidence trail + a decision that refuses to say SHIP.
- Not a fit if: they need production/security certification, deployment, or multi-tenant SaaS today.

## Persona B — "Solo/non-technical founder shipping via AI"
- Pain: cannot read diffs/logs; needs a plain-English "is this real and safe?" signal.
- Value hypothesis: the no-terminal operator dashboard + honest UNKNOWN/HOLD states.
- Not a fit if: they expect a hosted product or customer data handling now.

## Persona C — "Reviewer/auditor of AI-generated work"
- Pain: needs a reproducible, tamper-evident record of what an agent did and tested.
- Value hypothesis: sealed receipts + independent Checker/Gate Advisor separation.

All personas are synthetic. No real prospect list exists or is created by this preparation.
