# Objections & Responses (truthful)
**"Is this production-ready?"** → "No, and I won't claim it is. It's an early internal demo. What's real today is the governance and the honest evidence — proven on local runs."

**"Can it work on our private/production repo?"** → "Not during this phase. Public, non-sensitive repos only — no secrets, no private data. That boundary is deliberate."

**"Is my code/data safe?"** → "Nothing leaves your machine except the evidence note. No secrets requested, no external sends, no analytics. If your repo has anything sensitive, I stop and decline."

**"What do I actually get?"** → "A plain-English evidence note: exact commit, changed files, tests, an honest ship/do-not-ship/insufficient decision, and the limitations — plus a 20-minute walkthrough."

**"What if it can't do the task?"** → "Then you get an honest 'insufficient' — which is a real result. It's built to refuse to fake a success."

**"Is it secure / compliant / certified?"** → "No — I make no security or compliance claims. This is a governed demo, not an audit."

**"What does it cost?"** → "The first concierge run is free. Any pricing later is just a hypothesis I want your honest read on — I have no validated pricing and no customers yet."
