# What NOT To Do (protect yourself + stay honest)
- ❌ Don't say production-ready, secure, compliant, certified, or "we have customers/revenue." None are true.
- ❌ Don't quote a price as if it's set. Pricing is a hypothesis — ask, don't state.
- ❌ Don't run on a **private / production / customer** repo, or anything with secrets. Public, non-sensitive only.
- ❌ Don't promise deployment, a merge into their repo, or a security review.
- ❌ Don't hand the SAMPLE evidence note to a prospect as if it were *their* result — it's a format demo.
- ❌ Don't crop the "INTERNAL DEMO ONLY" banner out of any screenshot.
- ❌ Don't fill the prospect list with invented names.
- ❌ Don't authorize a real AI build/spend, or send outreach, without deciding it explicitly in the moment — those are your gates.
- ❌ Don't batch-message ten people. One warm contact first.
