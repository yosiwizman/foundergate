# Who To Contact First

Pick the person who scores highest on ALL of these:
1. **You already know them** and they'd give you honest feedback.
2. They **build software** and have a **public repo** they won't mind being a guinea pig.
3. **Low stakes** — an honest "insufficient" wouldn't bother them.
4. They can do a **quick 20-minute call** this week.
5. They **don't expect** a finished product, deployment, or security guarantees.

**Rule of one:** contact ONE person first. Not a batch. Learn from that conversation, then move to the next warmest name.

**Avoid first:** anyone who'd only offer a private/production repo, needs security/compliance sign-off, or expects a polished SaaS. (See DISQUALIFYING_PROSPECTS.md.)
