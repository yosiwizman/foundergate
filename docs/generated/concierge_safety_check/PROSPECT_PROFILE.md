# Prospect Profile (who this is for)

**Best-fit early prospect (hypothesis — no real prospects exist yet):**
- Small team or solo builder who already ships code and is **curious about autonomous AI dev** but wary of "the AI went rogue."
- Has at least one **public, non-sensitive repo** they'd happily use as a sandbox.
- Values **auditability and honesty** over flashy demos — the pitch is "proof it stays in its guardrails," not "magic."
- Comfortable being an early design partner: willing to give feedback, accept an honest "do-not-ship," and not expect production support.

**Where they hang out (channels to try — see OUTREACH):** dev-tool communities, indie-hacker / OSS maintainer circles, AI-agent builder groups, your existing personal network.

**Not a fit:** anyone who needs production deployment, security/compliance sign-off, private/customer data handling, or a finished SaaS today. (See DISQUALIFYING_PROSPECTS.)
