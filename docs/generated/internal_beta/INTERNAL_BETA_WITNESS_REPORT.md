4. Proven by endurance: L1-L4 (2h/5h/8h) autonomous, duration from disk.
5. Proven by real provider: 2 bounded claude -p runs under cap/paths/evidence.
6. Proven by real project: a working local Evidence Note project (36/36).
7. Unproven: production, customer, security, compliance, real data, deployment, scale.
8. Ready for INTERNAL demo: YES (with limits). 9. Ready for PAID customers: NO. 10. Next gate: concierge design-partner pilot (manual, non-sensitive).
