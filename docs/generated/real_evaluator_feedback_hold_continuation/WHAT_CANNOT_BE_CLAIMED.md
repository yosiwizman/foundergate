# What CANNOT Be Claimed (unless the evaluator actually says it later)
None of the following may be claimed now or implied, because no evaluator words exist:
- credible
- understood
- interested
- approved
- rejected
- buyer interest
- market signal
- customer validation
- paid demand
- commercial readiness
Additionally forbidden: outside validation, production readiness, security/compliance, SaaS readiness. A blank form is the ABSENCE of a signal — it is neither positive nor negative. It must not be spun either way.
