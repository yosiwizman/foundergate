# Claims Matrix (strict)
## 1. Allowed now (evidence-backed)
- "FounderGate ran fully autonomous endurance proofs (2h/5h/8h) with duration proven from disk logs, not a timer."
- "It merges work behind an explicit founder gate; it never merges or ships without the founder's word."
- "It ran real AI-provider builds under a hard cost cap, path limits, and evidence capture, then made a conservative honest decision (it refused to fabricate a SHIP)."
- "A founder can open a local golden-path screen showing honest UNKNOWN/STALE states and a visible provider-activation gate."
## 2. Allowed WITH LIMITS
- "There is a working local beta spine and a real-provider proof — as an internal, local demo (not production, not customer-facing)."
## 3. FORBIDDEN (must never claim)
- production-ready · customer-ready · final beta · secure · compliant · deployed · SaaS ready · arbitrary-repo support · customer-data-safe · investor-guaranteed · revenue-guaranteed · "merge = shipped".
## 4. Requires more proof
- real backend/data wiring; multi-customer; unattended real-provider at scale; security review; any production surface.
## 5. Investor-safe phrasing
- "An evidence-first governance layer for autonomous AI development runs — with proof it stays inside its guardrails." (Not "an AI that ships production software.")
## 6. Dangerous overclaims to avoid
- calling the demo "the product," implying paying customers, implying deployment, implying security/compliance.
## 7. Plain English (founder)
- Say: "We proved the *governance and honesty* works — cheaply and safely — on local demos. We have NOT proven it's ready for real customers or production, and we won't claim that."
