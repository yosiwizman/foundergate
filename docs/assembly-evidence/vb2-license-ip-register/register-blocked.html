License / IP register

Which components — dependencies, copied code, worker/AI-tool outputs — are recorded, and do they clear a commercial or pilot claim? A claim is shown clear only when every recorded entry has a known license, confirmed commercial use, no high or unknown copyleft/redistribution risk, an APPROVED status, and a review on record. A single unknown, high-risk, or unreviewed entry blocks the whole claim; an empty register is unsubstantiated, never clear. Nothing here adds, reviews, or approves an entry.

Any open-source worker, copied component, or AI tool used by FounderGate must enter the License/IP Register before real use. Unknown license/IP risk blocks commercial/pilot claims. Human legal review is required for high-risk or unclear licenses. The register is a concrete .foundergate/license_ip_register.json artifact, not just a policy.
