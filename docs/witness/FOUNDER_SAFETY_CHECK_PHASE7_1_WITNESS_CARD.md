# Founder Witness — Safety Check (Phase 7.1) — one page

**Witness candidate, not an accepted product.** You check whether it is clear and usable — not code, security, Git, Docker, pricing, or legal.

1. Double-click **FounderGate Safety Check** (on your Desktop). *(No terminal.)*
2. Confirm the initial page has **no completed result**.
3. Click **Use evaluation fixture**.
4. Confirm the fixture URL is visible.
5. Click **Run Safety Check**.
6. Confirm a **new Run ID** appears (e.g. `sc-…`).
7. Watch the **real stages** progress.
8. Confirm results stay **pending** while the runtime is active.
9. Confirm the **exact version** and **repository** appear together.
10. Confirm **Compileability observed**.
11. Confirm **Build not proven**.
12. Confirm **Tests not proven**.
13. Confirm **Security not proven**.
14. Confirm **Production readiness not proven**.
15. Confirm cleanup + deletion complete.
16. Click **Download Evidence Pack**.
17. Confirm the packet downloads.
18. Open the Evidence Pack summary.
19. Enter a **different** public URL → confirm **HELD**.
20. Enter an **SSH/private** URL → confirm **REFUSED**.
21. Find **Pause & get help**.
22. Judge whether it is understandable without technical help.
23. **Accept as a witness candidate**, reject, or report a problem.

You are NOT asked to use Terminal, run Node, inspect code/Docker/logs/Git, or approve security, production readiness, adoption, customer use, pricing, or legal terms.
