> a permanent extension of Test Before Founder. For every founder-facing UI change or witness
> candidate, before the founder is involved, FounderGate must complete one full foreground-visible
> browser run through the same launcher, browser, route and DOM interaction path the founder will use.
> A browser launched with headless=false is not sufficient by itself. The system must prove that the
> browser window was: visibly rendered; frontmost; onscreen; not minimized; on the active logged-in
> Mac graphical session; using the configured founder browser; launched through the real founder
> application or launcher; operated through actual DOM controls. Internal, unit, mutation, API and CI
> tests may run headlessly. The final pre-founder surface qualification may not. Headless exact-SHA CI
> remains separately required and may not substitute for foreground proof. Foreground proof may not
> substitute for exact-SHA CI. … Any weakening, removal, bypass or reclassification of this law
> requires: founder authorization; independent checking; mutation proof; exact-SHA CI; durable disk
> governance evidence. It does not authorize another live sandbox run, OpenSandbox adoption, main
> merge, customer or private repositories, production deployment, AKIOR access or M1 contact.
> Signed by the Founder and Acceptance Authority.
