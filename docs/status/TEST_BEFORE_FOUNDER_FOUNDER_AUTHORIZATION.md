# Founder Authorization — Test Before Founder (TBF-1)

Authoritative on-disk record. Recorded verbatim.

## Verbatim founder authorization
> FOUNDER AUTHORIZATION
>
> I authorize FounderGate to establish Test Before Founder as a permanent governing law.
>
> Every applicable machine-verifiable test, falsification check, recovery check, evidence check and
> independent review must be completed before work is presented to the founder.
>
> For UI-affecting work, browser rendering must be proven through actual browser automation and
> durable visual evidence before founder involvement.
>
> The founder must be used only for human perception, usability judgment, legal or business
> authority, signatures, live-action authorization, and other decisions that cannot be mechanically
> verified.
>
> This authorization does not claim that testing eliminates every possible defect.
>
> It requires FounderGate to exhaust all applicable machine-verifiable proof before interrupting the
> founder.
>
> This authorization does not accept the current Phase 7.1 interface, authorize main merge, adopt
> OpenSandbox, authorize customer or private repositories, authorize production deployment, access
> AKIOR, or contact the M1.
>
> Any future weakening, bypass, removal or material reclassification of this law requires:
> - a founder signature;
> - independent checker review;
> - mutation proof that the enforcement guard still fires;
> - exact-SHA CI;
> - a durable governance record.
>
> Signed by the Founder and Acceptance Authority.

## Scope this authorization DOES grant
Establishes Test Before Founder as permanent, disk-based, versioned, machine-enforced FounderGate law
(the TBF-1 policy at `.foundergate/policies/test_before_founder.json`, pointer, validators, schemas,
selftests, mutation proofs, CI). Reserves the founder for human perception, usability, signatures,
legal/commercial authority, and live-action authorization.

## Scope this authorization does NOT grant
NOT acceptance of the Phase 7.1 interface · NOT main merge · NOT OpenSandbox adoption · NOT customer or
private repositories · NOT production deployment · NOT AKIOR access · NOT M1 contact.

## Amendment protection
Any material change to TBF-1 requires all of: founder signature · independent checker · mutation proof
that the guard still fires · exact-SHA CI · updated policy digest · append-only governance record.
